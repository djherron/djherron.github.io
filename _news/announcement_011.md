In mid 2025, the [Alan Turing Institute](https://www.turing.ac.uk/) --- the UK's national institute for data science and artificial intelligence --- launched a [Defence & Security Grand Challenge](https://www.turing.ac.uk/work-turing/defence-security-grand-challenge-ai-security-call-proposals) that sought to commission several research projects relating to the theme of **AI Security**. I accepted an offer from Dr. Ernesto Jimenez-Ruiz, at City St George's, Univerity of London, to act as his **post-doctoral research assistant** on the project he proposed in response to the ATI's **call for proposals**.  Happily, Dr. Jimenez-Ruiz's project proposal was accepted by the ATI and the project was commissioned.

Our research pertains to the problem of **OWL ontology alignment**. Amongst other things, we are exploring ways of integrating **LLMs (large language models)** with the ontology matching system [LogMap](https://github.com/ernestojimenezruiz/logmap-matcher) in order to boost the quality of the ontology alignments that **LogMap** is capable of generating.
