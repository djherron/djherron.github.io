In January 2026 I received word that my thesis examiners had accepted my thesis amendments and had recommended the formal award of a PhD.  :tada:.

Shortly afterwards, I submitted my final thesis (with all of the markup for my amendments removed).

Soon after that, I received an **award letter**, dated 05 Feb 2026, from City St George's, University of London. The award letter said that: "Senate has approved that you be awarded a Doctor of Philosophy for your thesis on Neurosymbolic Learning and Reasoning with OWL-based Knowledge Graphs." :tada: :wine_glass:.

What a journey!
